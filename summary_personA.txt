The majority-class baseline predicts every patient as low-risk (class 0).

Naive Bayes with SMOTENC improves accuracy to 73.08% and high-risk recall to 56%.


13. FINAL CONCLUSION
--------------------------------------------------
Naive Bayes + SMOTENC was the stronger of the two classification
baselines on the held-out test set.

It achieved:
- 73.08% accuracy
- 56% recall for the high-risk class
- 0.59 high-risk F1
- 0.595 ROC-AUC

SMOTENC substantially improved the Naive Bayes baseline on this
particular train/test split, especially for detecting the high-risk
class.

Tumor Grade had the highest permutation importance among the three
clinical features.

Because the held-out test set contains only 26 patients, these
results should be treated as baseline results rather than definitive
predictive performance.
